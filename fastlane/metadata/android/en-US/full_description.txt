Sentry Bridge converts 1090ES and 978 MHz UAT traffic from a Sentry or Sentry Plus
in Compatibility Mode to GDL90 for compatible EFBs. It relays available GPS,
heartbeat and weather frames over the receiver's WiFi.

<ul>
<li>Run beside one EFB on the same Android device, or send to an EFB on another device.</li>
<li>Request Compatibility Mode again after receiver power cycles.</li>
<li>Receiver settings, backups, logs, capture analysis and a loopback simulator.</li>
<li>Local processing, with optional GitHub update notifications disabled by default.</li>
<li>Free software under GPLv3, with third-party license notices included.</li>
</ul>

Requires Android 8 or later and compatible receiver hardware. Same-device port
sharing depends on the EFB. Hardware coverage is limited; support is not established
for every EFB, device or firmware. EFB weather display remains unverified. AHRS,
CO, temperature, barometric sensor and battery data are unavailable in Compatibility
Mode, including to ForeFlight. Disable Power Savings in the receiver's web settings.

Advisory only; not certified for navigation or collision avoidance. It does not
replace see-and-avoid, required equipment or a weather briefing. The bridge does
not decrypt the encrypted stream or modify receiver firmware. Not affiliated with
or endorsed by uAvionix, ForeFlight or EFB vendors. Captures can contain flight tracks;
read Privacy in Settings before recording or sharing them.
