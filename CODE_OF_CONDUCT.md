# Code of conduct

Sentry Bridge is a small project run by volunteers. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else in the project, is expected to keep it a welcoming place.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, including with people new to aviation, software or English.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and explain your own decisions the same way.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Discrimination of any kind, including by age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
  gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
  identity and orientation.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, a tail number or a location,
  without their permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **c@tehcnological.com**. Reports are kept private, and you will get a reply.
Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens

The maintainer reviews each report and decides what to do: a private note, a public warning, or a
temporary or permanent ban from the project. Comments, commits, code and issues that break this code
may be edited or removed.
